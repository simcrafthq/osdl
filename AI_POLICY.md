# AI usage policy

AI tools may assist contributions to this project. Contributors remain
responsible for the work they submit.

External contributors must:

- Disclose the AI tool and the extent of its use.
- Understand each submitted change and how it interacts with the project.
- Review and edit AI-generated content before submission. Remove inaccurate or
  irrelevant content.

Maintainers may use AI tools at their discretion. They remain responsible for
the work they submit.

This policy is adapted from the
[Ghostty AI Usage Policy](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md).
